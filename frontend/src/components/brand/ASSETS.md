# Brand assets

- Liklet: the existing `public/images/logo.jpg` is the user-confirmed logo. Retain it and the existing favicon set.
- Google: `public/brand/google-g.png` was downloaded without modification from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png. Its original colours and proportions are retained in Google sign-in and the digital marketing navigation entry. Source and usage guidance: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
- Service navigation: `ServiceMark.tsx` uses the React mark for IT services, Instagram for social media, the original full-colour Google asset for digital marketing, and YouTube for video editing.
- Social platforms: standard Simple Icons and Font Awesome brand paths are supplied by the installed `react-icons` package. `PlatformMark.tsx` centralises their use; no emoji or generated substitute logos are used.
- Project images: the existing screenshots in `public/web projects/` are retained. No generated team portraits or customer avatars are used in the marketing pages.
- Homepage service cards: real photography is stored locally in `public/service-cards/` as 16:9 crops. Sources are [Nubelson Fernandes / Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/UcYBL5V0xWQ), [Detail .co / Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/HFnIAcvXETc), [Swello / Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/fDisDvdiqrQ), and [TourBox / Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/xGklNeRfBK8). Each source is marked free to use under the Unsplash License.

Lucide icons are used for functional controls such as menu toggles, chevrons, navigation, and account status. Decorative sparkle, lightning, and rocket badges are absent from rendered pages.
